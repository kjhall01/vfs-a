# Gibbs/entropy transport: an ocean research candidate

Parent: draft #35, `c3e9d6cdf39f4d978ed30cfb9dc22d660f7c4e39`.
This is an ocean-owned research implementation using the same physical
Hamiltonian and surface calorics as #35, with different prognostic coordinates,
face traces and limiting. All installed `vfs/` files, defaults, fixtures and
tolerances remain unchanged. ALE #32 is separate; keep the drafts unmerged.

The candidate addresses the entropy counterexample in #34/#35: entropy is
transported conservatively, and numerical heating can only add entropy.
Limiter work that cannot be supplied by released heat is paired with momentum.
A separate budget on every incident face makes that force correction vanish
when **one edge** crosses zero velocity even while its neighbors remain limited.
This is conditional on strictly interior active thermodynamic states and the
actual-carrier Euler restriction. It is not a claim of a production ocean,
physical turbulent mixing, local-extremum preservation or exact finite-step
energy conservation.

## Entropy as a stored content

The research state stores velocity u, thickness h, entropy content E=h*eta,
salt content Sigma=h*SP and nonnegative passive contents. Temperature recovery
is explicit through a bracketed analytic Newton solve; density remains JMD95.
The previous enthalpy-coordinate implementation and its falsifying tests stay
unchanged. The same physical state has the same energy in either coordinate.

This candidate requires `0 < SP < 42` and interior surface-reference
temperatures `-2.5 < theta90 < 40 degC`. Active freshwater at exactly SP=0 is
**not supported**: the entropy/chemical derivative is singular. No salt floor
is added. Zero passive tracers are supported and do not affect active limiting.
Entropy itself can be negative under its reference convention; it is not
subject to a positivity bound. Celsius temperatures can also be negative.

Let a(theta,S) and eta(theta,S) be the #35 surface enthalpy and entropy,
T=theta90+273.15 K, and m=rho0*A. For

\[
H=H_d(u,h,h\theta(E/h,\Sigma/h),\Sigma)+\sum mh,a,
\]

write the old dynamic-energy covectors as g. Its transformed dynamic covectors
are

\[
q=g_\Theta/\eta_\theta,\quad
H^d_E=q,\quad H^d_\Sigma=g_\Sigma-q\eta_S,\quad
H^d_h=g_h+g_\Theta\theta+q(S\eta_S-\eta).
\]

The full covectors add `m*T`, `m*mu` and `m*b` respectively, where
`mu=a_S-T*eta_S` and `b=a-T*eta-S*mu=G-S*mu`. The velocity covector is unchanged.
All derivatives are analytic; independent autograd is used only in tests and
the independent pressure reference. No tendency is generated by forward AD.

Total physical entropy is simply `sum(m*E)`. Thus conservative entropy fluxes
have zero global contribution regardless of their chosen face trace, and a
nonnegative entropy source gives a direct, inspectable production ledger.

## Ocean-specific Gibbs face identity

Using arithmetic entropy/salt traces in these coordinates would create a
spurious reference-caloric pressure. Instead, the high traces must satisfy

\[
\Delta b+\eta^*\Delta T+S^*\Delta\mu=0.
\]

For any potential P(T,S), define symmetric coordinate-increment derivatives:
`D_T P` averages the temperature secant at the two endpoint salinities, and
`D_S P` averages the salinity secant at the two endpoint temperatures. Then
`Delta P=D_T P*Delta T+D_S P*Delta S` exactly in real arithmetic.

With `mu=G_S`, use

\[
d=(\overline\mu-D_SG)/(D_S\mu),\qquad
S^*=\overline S+d,\qquad \eta^*=-D_TG-dD_T\mu.
\]

Indeed, `Delta b=Delta G-Sbar*Delta mu-mubar*Delta S`; substitution and the
discrete derivative identity for mu cancel every remaining term. Positive
finite `D_S mu` is checked. The formulation is symmetric under swapping the
endpoints and has the correct equal-state limit.

`ocean_gibbs_faces.py` evaluates polynomial secants by Horner/factorization,
and logarithmic secants with their analytic equal-state limit. It does not
divide by a small temperature jump, salinity jump or velocity. The positive
salinity restriction remains necessary. The means are thermodynamic traces,
not a guarantee that every trace lies between its endpoint values.

For the dynamic covectors, the centered force is

\[
\dot u=-G(H^d_h/m)-\eta^*G(H^d_E/m)-S^*G(H^d_\Sigma/m)
       +\dot u_{rotation}.
\]

The reference-caloric force cancels by the Gibbs identity and is removed
analytically before evaluation. The dynamic pressure remains the negative
adjoint of the corresponding scalar fluxes. Rotation uses the existing
kinetic-metric skew operator; existing rotation/kinetic-consistency limits
are not claimed resolved.

## Actual-carrier ledger and individual-face continuity

Use the existing ocean donor reference with the actual full Euler carrier
`F=dv*I(h)*u`, its outgoing-volume restriction and thickness reserve. For salt,
correct that reference toward `dt*F*S*`. Passive high traces remain arithmetic.
Salt is bounded by 0 and 42; passives by zero. This is **admissibility limiting**,
not the local-extremum FCT of #33. Temperature/entropy extrema and passive
upper bounds are not guaranteed.

At a node with d incident active faces and a remaining lower or upper amount
capacity B, allocate at most `B/d` to each face. A face correction demand D is
multiplied by the minimum of one and its endpoint allowances divided by D.
Summing all incident allowances gives B, so all outgoing corrections fit the
same authoritative amount budget. Unused shares are not redistributed: this
simple sufficient construction can be more diffusive near a bound.

The finite upper bound reserves floating-point room, capped at half the
available capacity so a strictly positive capacity stays positive. This only
reduces an antidiffusive transfer; it never clips or floors stored content.
Final amount checks reject floating-point failures. Dry isolated nodes have
no faces and remain unchanged.

The reason for per-face allowances is stronger than whole-flow rest. With a
common node ratio, one nearly stationary edge can inherit a finite limiting
factor from its moving neighbors. If its correction needs mechanical work,
the force can jump at zero velocity. In the retained counter-oriented salt/
geometry stress case, an intermediate common-ratio construction approached
about +9.34e-11 and -9.34e-11 m/s2 from the two velocity signs while returning
zero exactly at rest. Those intermediate formulas are not the candidate.

With the new allocation, strict donor salinity/capacity gives B>0 even while
other faces remain limited. As one edge's velocity goes to zero, its own
demand D goes to zero, so its beta becomes exactly one. Both its trace change
and its force correction then vanish. No velocity floor, smoothing scale or
division by velocity is used. This is a pointwise interior-state result, not
a uniform limit as salinity simultaneously approaches zero or a domain bound.

## Entropy correction and work allocation

Salt limiting gives `delta_S=(1-beta)*(S_donor-S*)`. Following the atmospheric
composition-entropy construction, attach

\[
\delta\eta=\overline{(\partial\eta/\partial S)_{T}}\,\delta S
\]

relative to the Gibbs high trace. The coefficient is the arithmetic endpoint
mean of the analytic composition derivative. This is an explicit numerical
choice inspired by the atmosphere; it is not a uniquely determined physical
mixing closure. Independently blending entropy toward its donor trace would
unnecessarily upwind heat where the actual salt change is zero and caused a
failed directional-derivative experiment. The candidate attaches entropy to
the actual composition change instead. Passive limiters are independent and
never enter this thermodynamic work.

Let e denote the full energy covectors divided by area. The work change is

\[
w_f=F_f b_f,\qquad
b_f=\Delta e_E\,\delta\eta+\Delta e_\Sigma\,\delta S.
\]

For w<0, release `Q_f=-w_f` as nonnegative heat, half at each endpoint,
dividing by its full positive `H_E` to obtain the entropy-content source.
For w>0, use `delta_u=-b_f/(rho0*dc)` on that edge/layer. Since
`H_u=rho0*dc*F`, its mechanical work is exactly -w. At w=0 both corrections
are zero. Hence spatial total energy closes and

\[
\dot{\mathcal S}=\sum_f\frac{Q_f}{2}
       \left(\frac1{T_{B,i}}+\frac1{T_{B,j}}\right)\ge0,
\qquad T_B=H_E/m>0.
\]

Heat placement is a declared equal-power allocation. It satisfies the laws
above but is not a calibrated turbulent diffusivity. Mechanical allocation
does not convert forbidden negative entropy production into artificial
cooling; it closes the corresponding scalar work through the momentum budget.

Euler installs the salt/passive/entropy amounts directly and adds the
nonnegative entropy source. Every full Euler endpoint is checked for the
joint caloric domain, including heating, before SSPRK3 convex combinations.
The salt budget alone does not guarantee that domain: an initially interior
hot salt front has a tested full-stage rejection. There is no automatic dt
retry, clipping or hidden heat reservoir.

Because entropy is a linear stored inventory, each accepted Euler stage and
the SSP convex combinations preserve its nonnegative production budget up to
roundoff. Total energy is nonlinear and retains temporal discretization error.
Potential-temperature and potential-enthalpy inventories are not separately
conserved under these numerical thermodynamic conversions.

## Verification and limits

The committed `entropy-results.json` reports ten closed/coastal front,
salt-front, tilted-layer, near-freshwater and near-upper-salinity cases. The
tilted case has zero production rather than the negative rate in #35. Salt
limiting is genuinely active in the boundary stress cases; the near-freshwater
case exercises heating and the counter-oriented upper-salinity case exercises
nonzero mechanical work. Independent AD of H checks the analytic covectors
and the complete RHS work, not only a self-reported diagnostic identity.

The entropy budget reports production separately from global subtraction
roundoff. A tiny negative difference of two extensive floating-point entropy
inventories is not relabelled as positive; its residual is reported relative
to the initial absolute inventory and checked against the exact source ledger.

Pressure is compared with an independent height integral/analytic horizontal
profile using the pre-existing EOS. Internal-wave checks repeat #31's
continuum two-layer reference for **0.05 of one period**, at 64 and 256 cells.
They test short evolution, not sustained propagation, wave growth or damping.
Temporal refinement uses dt=1000,500,250 s at fixed 1000 s duration.
No scientific accuracy threshold is introduced.

performance, long-rollout and coupled-model qualification remain open.

## Reproduce and proceed

Build/install the wheel, then copy `tests`, `tools` and `examples` outside the
checkout. The new forward code uses PyTorch and the committed #35 coefficients.
The separate inherited GSW reference check requires GSW 3.6.23/Python >=3.11.

```sh
python -I -m pytest tests --fixture-mode=portable -q
python -I tools/probe_ocean_entropy.py --output entropy-results.json --waves
```

Hosted CI requires the new driver without the longer wave runs; the committed
local report includes both waves. No original historical fixture or tolerance
is replaced.
