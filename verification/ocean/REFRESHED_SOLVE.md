# Refreshed end-TKE budget acceptance

`ConsistentPathClosure` is an opt-in research closure using JMD95 conservative-
path work, positive coupled iteration and a fresh nonlinear budget check. It
remains outside the installed package. Supported ocean defaults, physical
sources, EOS, energy allocation, fixtures and budget tolerances are unchanged.

## Equations and acceptance

For a candidate end TKE k, recompute native momentum diffusion and its energy
production P(k), conservative scalar diffusion and buoyancy work B(k), TKE
transport F(k), and dissipation coefficient d(k), from the same initial state.
The local residual is

    R(k) = M*(k-k0) - P(k) + B(k) + d(k)*k - D(F(k)).

The unchanged acceptance scale is

    M*1e-13 + 1e-9*(M*k0 + P(k) + abs(B(k))).

Candidates must be finite and nonnegative, satisfy the existing iterate-change
and frozen-budget checks, and additionally satisfy abs(R)/scale<=1 after the
complete fresh trial. The accepted momentum/scalar state, buoyancy work,
transport and dissipated heat all come from this refreshed trial. The final
actual-H and entropy checks remain in force. A refreshed NaN/Inf is rejected.
No clipping, TKE floor, compensating heat or weaker tolerance is introduced.

The positive source/sink fixed-point iteration and native tridiagonal solves
remain. An optional Anderson acceleration uses the dimensionless nonlinear
budget residual to choose its secant mixing coefficient, instead of the prior
absolute TKE-change norm. Existing positivity, bounded-extrapolation and finite
coefficient safeguards remain. This is residual-weighted positive iteration,
not Newton convergence or a guarantee of a solution for every input.

The research candidate allows at most 320 iterations; the parent prototype's
80-iteration default remains reproducible. `refresh_budget=False` and
`budget_acceleration=False` retain the legacy research path. The current legacy
one-hour run matches the saved profiles, energy residual and iteration ledger
exactly. Full thermodynamic trial counts are reported for the new path, including
candidate checks, so iteration counts alone do not hide their extra cost.

## Reproduction

Use an installed wheel outside the checkout with copies of validation, tests,
tools and examples:

```sh
OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python validation/ocean_column/consistent_matrix.py --output results
python validation/ocean_column/analyze_consistent.py --input results --output summary.json
python validation/ocean_column/benchmark_consistent.py --output benchmark.json
python -I -m pytest tests --fixture-mode=portable -q
```

The matrix compares legacy, refreshed-only and budget-accelerated acceptance,
then timestep, resolution, cooling, rain/heating, quadrature and twelve-hour
controls. Campaign wall times overlap other jobs and are not isolated benchmarks.
Only completed equal-time profiles are compared. A finer timestep is a numerical
reference, not an analytic solution or proof of temporal convergence.

## Scope

CPU float64 column evidence only. No horizontal dycore, GPU, long-backward,
dry-cell or coupled atmosphere-ocean qualification. Scalar transport and all
coupled dynamics still require broader timestep and vertical-resolution tests.
The fresh guard addresses end-coefficient consistency; it does not by itself
establish physical closure calibration, entrainment accuracy or unconditional
nonlinear convergence.

## Short controls and pinned rejection replay

All ten one-hour controls complete: legacy, refreshed-only and budget-accelerated
16-layer dt300 runs; budget-accelerated dt150/dt75/dt600, 32 layers, cooling,
rain/heating and order-5 path quadrature. Maximum absolute energy residual across
these cases is 4.42e-11 J/kg.

The saved six-hour rejected input passes both refreshed variants at iteration
92. Independent fresh-oracle residual/limit is 0.76221 with the prior acceleration
metric and 0.75535 with the budget metric. No accepted candidate is clipped.
`consistent_replay.py` reproduces this using the trusted checkpoint generated by
`diagnose_budget.py`; the pickle is not uploaded.

For the one-hour dt300 run, refreshed-only uses 259 iterations / 345 full trials;
budget acceleration uses 206 / 256. These counts include fresh acceptance trials.
The two endpoints differ by 1.38e-10 in velocity relative L2, 1.50e-8 in TKE
relative L2 and 1.99e-11 C temperature RMS. Relative to legacy, the new default
research candidate differs by 9.47e-9 in velocity, 1.43e-7 in TKE and 3.12e-9 C
in temperature RMS. Small bulk endpoint differences do not excuse a failed local
nonlinear residual. Order 3 versus 5 has TKE relative difference 6.39e-16 in this
one-hour case; this is not a universal quadrature-error bound.

## Fixed-input cost

Three interleaved repeats after warm-up, one thread, 16 layers, dt150:

| Candidate | Iterations | Full trials | Median process CPU seconds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Legacy | 18 | 18 | 1.779 |
| Refreshed, prior acceleration | 25 | 34 | 3.501 |
| Refreshed, budget acceleration | 25 | 34 | 3.359 |

The stricter solve costs about 1.89 times the legacy CPU time on this input.
Both refreshed variants do the same number of trials here; their timing difference
is not evidence of an algorithmic speedup. Budget acceleration reduces trials
over the one-hour trajectory (256 versus 345), but not for every step. Concurrent
campaign jobs ran during this benchmark, so process CPU samples and wall samples
are both retained and no isolated wall-throughput claim is made.

## Regression

Installed unchanged wheel outside checkout, portable suite: **1023 passed,
one CUDA skip**, one existing tensor-to-scalar warning, 276.47 s. Three new tests
verify refreshed residual/work/native momentum agreement, positivity, entropy,
full-H closure and transactional rejection. Corrected-Gibbs and dry-helper
portable fixture comparisons pass. Legacy saved-profile comparison is exact.

## Twelve-hour result

The 16-layer, dt300, budget-accelerated wind run **completes all 12 hours**
(144 accepted steps), beyond the legacy six-hour rejection. Final full-energy
residual is 1.60e-11 J/kg. Every committed step passes the refreshed local budget,
positivity and final actual-H/entropy checks. No timestep rejection is hidden by
a retry, clipping, a floor or relaxed budget tolerance.

The run uses 7637 iterations and 8381 full thermodynamic trials; recorded wall
time is 869.65 s with other campaign/regression jobs overlapping part of it.
Cost is intermittent: 1687 iterations from hours 6–7, 2448 from 7–8, 376 from
8–9, 256 from 9–10, 660 from 10–11 and 601 from 11–12. Completion establishes
more robustness in this case, not an efficient or generally convergent closure.

Next qualify longer trajectories and finer-step/finer-layer references while
improving the positive nonlinear iteration, particularly during weak-TKE
transitions. Keep the fresh-residual acceptance contract as the correctness
reference for those changes. Twelve hours is not 48-hour, climate-run, GPU or
full-dycore qualification, and the substantial TKE timestep differences remain.
